How to use the Thumbnail Prompt Generator

Enter your video topic and click Generate Prompt. You can use the generated description as a starting point when designing your thumbnail or working with an image-generation tool.

Tips for better YouTube thumbnails

  • Focus on one clear visual idea.
  • Keep thumbnail text short and readable.
  • Use strong visual contrast.
  • Make the design match the actual video.
  • Avoid overcrowding the thumbnail.
