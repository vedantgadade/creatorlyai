How to use the Ad Copy Generator

Enter the product, service or offer you want to promote and click Generate Ad Copy. The tool creates a simple starting structure that you can edit for your advertising platform and audience.

Tips for better ad copy

  • Clearly explain what you are offering.
  • Focus on a useful benefit for the audience.
  • Keep the message simple and easy to understand.
  • Use a clear call to action.
  • Make sure your claims are accurate and supported.
